  • An overgrown planet that visibly changes with every sweep.
  • Haircuts, falling wool and increasingly powerful clippers.
  • Hidden planetary discoveries and downloadable result cards.

How to play

Hold and drag to shave. Rotate using right-drag, arrow keys, two fingers on touch, or the on-screen buttons. Keyboard players can hold S to shave at the center. Spend wool on upgrades. Finish all five clients. Progress is saved in the browser.
