Your haircut stays on your device.

Updated November 8, 2026.

Planet Barber stores your game progress and preferences in your browser's local storage. The game does not send that save to a game server. Use the reset control in settings to clear game progress, or clear this site's browser storage to remove all locally stored data.

The game contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics, account registration, payment collection or email signup form. Hosting providers may process ordinary technical request data such as IP addresses and browser information to serve and protect the website under their own policies.

Sharing is initiated by you. A share action can open your device's sharing interface or copy a game link; saving a result card downloads an image. Neither action automatically posts to a social network.

If you email us, your email is processed by the mail provider and used to respond to your enquiry. Do not include sensitive information in gameplay feedback.
